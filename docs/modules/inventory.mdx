---
title: "Inventory"
description: "Track school assets and consumables — categories, SKUs, condition, purchase info, suppliers and warranties."
---

Open **Inventory** from the sidebar to see your items, then **Add** a new one.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Item details">
    Enter the **name**, **category**, **SKU**, and **unit of measure**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Condition & stock">
    Record the condition and current stock level.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Purchase & supplier">
    Capture purchase information, **supplier**, and **warranty** details. Optionally upload a photo.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Active status">
    Keep items **active in inventory**, or archive them when retired — archived items stay in your records.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
Use the search and filters on the inventory list to find items quickly across large asset registers.
</Info>
