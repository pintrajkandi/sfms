## Transport

Open **Transport** from the sidebar to manage:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Routes & vehicles" icon="bus">
    Define routes and the vehicles that serve them.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Riders & fees" icon="user-group">
    Link students to routes for transport fees.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Expenses" icon="fuel-pump">
    Record fuel and maintenance against a route.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Profitability" icon="chart-line">
    See income vs. expense per route.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
When you enrol a student, you can link them to a transport route so their transport fee is tracked with the rest of their fees.
</Tip>

## Hostel
