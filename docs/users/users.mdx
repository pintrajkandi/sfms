---
title: "Adding Users"
description: "Invite staff to your school workspace and manage their accounts."
---

Users are the staff members who sign in to your school workspace — administrators, accountants, front-office staff and so on. They are separate from **students** and **teachers** (which are records you manage, not necessarily login accounts).

## Add a staff user

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Users / Team area">
    Administrators can manage staff accounts for the school.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the account">
    Enter the person's **name**, **email** and assign a **role** (see [Roles & permissions](/users/roles-permissions)).
  </Step>
  <Step title="They verify and sign in">
    New users confirm their email, then sign in from your school's subdomain. See [Logging in](/getting-started/logging-in).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
The first admin is created automatically when you [register your school](/getting-started/sign-up). That admin can add more users.
</Info>

## Managing accounts

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Changing someone's role">
    Update the user's role to grant or restrict what they can see and do. Changes take effect on their next action.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Deactivating a user">
    Remove access for staff who have left. Their historical actions (e.g. payments they recorded) remain in your records.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Password resets">
    Users reset their own password from the **Forgot password** link on the sign-in screen.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Warning>
Only trusted administrators should have access to add or change users. Roles determine access to money-sensitive areas like payments and payouts.
</Warning>
