YukiCares helps schools **automate fee collection, accounting, expense management and financial reporting** — everything your front office and accountant need, in one secure platform.

Each school runs on its own private workspace (subdomain). Your data is never shared with any other school.

## New here?

    Create your workspace and verify your email — see [Sign up](/getting-started/sign-up).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete your settings">
