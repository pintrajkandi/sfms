---
title: "Collect a Fee"
description: "Record a student fee payment with the step-by-step wizard, choose a payment method, and generate a numbered receipt."
---

Open **Fee Collection** from the sidebar. The page shows your collection totals and a searchable list of **fees collected**. To record a new payment, choose **+ New Collection**.

## The collection wizard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Student Info">
    Search for the student by name, ID or phone and select them. Their details and outstanding balance load automatically.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fee Details">
    Add one or more **fee types** and enter the amount for each. The wizard totals them into the amount due.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Payment">
    Choose the **payment method**, confirm the amount and date, and add an optional reference:

    <CardGroup cols={3}>
      <Card title="Cash" icon="money-bill" />
      <Card title="Bank Transfer" icon="building-columns" />
      <Card title="UPI" icon="mobile-screen" />
    </CardGroup>

    For non-cash methods you can record a **reference / UTR number**. Then choose **Confirm Payment**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
A **numeric receipt number** is generated automatically when you confirm — you don't type it in. The payment is recorded against a new invoice for the student.
</Info>

## Payment methods

| Method | Reference captured |
| --- | --- |
| **Cash** | None needed |
| **Bank Transfer** | UTR / voucher number (optional) |
| **UPI** | UPI transaction reference (optional) |

<Tip>
Payments are **idempotent** — if you accidentally submit twice, the student is not double-charged.
</Tip>

## Reviewing collected fees

Back on the **Fee Collection** page, the **Fees Collected** list shows every payment with its date, receipt number, student, method, amount and status. Use the search box to find a specific receipt, student or reference, and open the **Invoice** or **Receipt** for any row.

<Note>
There's no separate "Collections" menu — the full list of collected fees lives right here on the Fee Collection page.
</Note>
