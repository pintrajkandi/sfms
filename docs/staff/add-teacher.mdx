---
title: "Add a Teacher"
description: "Register a teacher with their personal details, salary structure and bank account — the data payroll and payslips rely on."
---

Open **Add Teacher** from the sidebar (or the **+ Add Teacher** button on the Teachers list).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Personal information">
    Enter **first name**, **last name**, email, gender, date of birth and photo.

    <Warning>
    **Phone number** must be **numeric and exactly 10 digits**, and must be unique across your teachers.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Employment details">
    These fields are **required**:
    - **Department** (from [Departments](/settings/departments), or typed in)
    - **Joining date**

    Optionally add employment type, experience and qualifications.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Class assignments">
    Add the classes this teacher handles, with a role such as *Class Teacher*, *Assistant Teacher* or *Coordinator*.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Salary structure (monthly)">
    Enter the **basic** salary (required, must be greater than zero) plus HRA, medical and other allowances, and standard deductions (PF, TDS, other). The form shows an estimated net.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Bank account details">
    These are **required** so payouts can be paid out:
    - **Account holder name**
    - **Bank name**
    - **Account number**
    - **IFSC code**
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save">
    An employee ID is generated automatically. The teacher now appears in the Teachers list and can be paid via [Payouts](/staff/payouts).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Required fields are marked with an asterisk and validated on save. If something's missing or the phone isn't 10 digits, you'll see inline errors.
</Note>
