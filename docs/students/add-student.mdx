---
title: "Add a Student"
description: "Enrol a student, capture their details, and assign their annual fee structure for accurate paid-vs-outstanding tracking."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Students → Add Student">
    Open the enrolment form. You can also save a draft and finish later.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Personal information">
    Enter **first name**, **last name**, **date of birth** and **gender**. Optionally upload a photo. First name, last name, DOB and gender are required.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Contact information">
    Add the student's contact and the **guardian** details (name, relation, phone, email). Phone numbers must be 10 digits.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Academic information">
    Pick the **class** and **section** (from [Classes & Sections](/settings/classes-sections)), the **enrollment date**, and status. Class and enrollment date are required.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fee structure (annual)">
    Enter the student's assigned annual fees:
    - **School fee**
    - **Tuition fee**
    - **Transport fee**
    - **Other fee**

    The form shows a live **total annual fee**. These amounts drive the student's paid-vs-outstanding across the app.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Add Student**. A unique student ID is generated automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
The **fee structure** is what makes fee collection meaningful: YukiCares treats the total annual fee as what the student owes, subtracts everything they've paid, and shows the **outstanding** balance on the student and collection screens.
</Tip>

<Info>
You can edit any student later — including their fee structure — and the outstanding figure recalculates automatically.
</Info>

## Importing many students

If you're onboarding a whole school, you can bulk-import students from a spreadsheet on the Students page instead of adding them one by one. Review the import result summary for any rows that need attention.
