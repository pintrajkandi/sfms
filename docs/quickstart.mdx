---
title: "Quick Start"
description: "Get your school live on YukiCares in eight steps — from registration to your first receipt."
---

This is the fastest path from a brand-new account to collecting your first fee. Each step links to a detailed guide.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register your school">
    Go to the sign-up page, enter your school name, admin email and password. You'll get your own workspace at `your-school.yukicares.cloud`.

    [Full guide →](/getting-started/sign-up)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify your email">
    Open the verification link we email you. Until you verify, login is blocked.

    <Tip>Didn't get it? Use **Resend verification** on the login screen.</Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete your school profile">
    On first login you're taken straight to **Settings**. Enter at least your **school name** and save — this unlocks the rest of the app.

    [School profile →](/settings/school-profile)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set up classes & sections">
    Add the classes and sections you run (e.g. *Class 1 → A, B*). These power the student and fee screens.

    [Classes & sections →](/settings/classes-sections)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Define your fee structure">
    Create your fee types (Tuition, Transport, Lab, etc.) so they're ready to bill.

    [Fee setup →](/settings/fee-setup)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add students">
    Enrol students and record their assigned annual fees (school, tuition, transport, other) so the app can track paid vs outstanding.

    [Add a student →](/students/add-student)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add teachers & staff">
    Register teachers with their salary structure and bank details.

    [Add a teacher →](/staff/add-teacher)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Collect your first fee">
    Use the **Fee Collection** wizard to record a payment. A numbered receipt is generated automatically, ready to print on your school letterhead.

    [Collect a fee →](/fees/collect-fee)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Prefer to work on your phone? YukiCares installs as an app on Android, iPhone and desktop — see [Use on mobile](/mobile-app).
</Note>
