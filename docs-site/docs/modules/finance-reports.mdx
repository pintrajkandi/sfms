## Finance & Accounting

The **Finance** and **Accounting** sections give you the money picture:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Income vs. expense" icon="scale-balanced">
    Track collections against spending over time.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Expense breakdown" icon="chart-pie">
    See where money goes by category and cost center.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Net savings trend" icon="chart-line">
    Watch your surplus/deficit month over month.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Transactions ledger" icon="book">
    A complete ledger of financial activity, with accounting journals, trial balance, P&L and balance sheet.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Reports

Open **Reports** for ready-made analytics you can filter and export (CSV / XLSX):

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Defaulters & Aging" icon="hourglass-half">
    Outstanding fees bucketed by how overdue they are.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Predictive Risk" icon="wand-magic-sparkles">
    Students ranked by likelihood of paying late.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Teacher Payouts" icon="money-check-dollar">
    Salary & incentive payouts, filterable by status.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Payments Ledger" icon="receipt">
    Every recorded payment for the period.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Collection Analysis" icon="chart-column">
    Collections by class, by employee and by method.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
