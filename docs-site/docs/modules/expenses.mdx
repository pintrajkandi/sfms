---
title: "Expenses"
description: "Log and categorise school spending, attach receipts, and capture method-specific payment references."
---

Open **Expenses** from the sidebar to record a new expense.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Basic information">
    Enter the **title**, **category**, and **expense date** (using the calendar picker).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Amount & payment">
    Enter the **amount** and choose a **payment method**. Depending on the method, a matching detail field appears:

    | Method | Detail captured |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Card / Company Card** | Card last 4 digits |
    | **Cheque** | Cheque number |
    | **UPI** | UPI ID |
    | **Bank Transfer** | Voucher / UTR number |

    Mark the expense **reimbursable** if it needs to be paid back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Additional details">
    Add the **vendor**, a **project / cost center**, and any notes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach a receipt">
    Upload a JPG, PNG or PDF receipt (optional).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    The expense is saved and flows into your finance reports. You can also **Save as Draft** to finish later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Consistent **categories** and **cost centers** make your expense breakdown and P&L far more useful.
</Tip>
