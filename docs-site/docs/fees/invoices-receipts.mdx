## Receipts

Every recorded payment produces a **receipt** with an automatic numeric receipt number.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the receipt">
    From the Fee Collection list or an invoice, choose **Receipt**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Print or save as PDF">
    Click **Print / Save PDF**. The receipt prints as a school **letterhead** — your logo, name and address at the top — followed by the payment details, student, amount, method and a verification QR/barcode.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
For the letterhead to look official, upload your **logo** and fill in your **address** under [Branding & Contact settings](/settings/school-profile). Without them, the header falls back to a plain style.
</Warning>

## Invoices

Open **Invoices** from the sidebar to see all invoices. Each invoice shows the student, fee breakdown, totals, amount paid and balance, with your school details as a letterhead.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Print / Download" icon="print">
    Print or save any invoice as a PDF — it uses the same branded letterhead.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Pay online" icon="credit-card">
    If online payments are enabled for your school, a **Pay Online** button lets guardians pay a balance via UPI/card.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
![invoices-receipts image](/img/invoices-receipts.png)
## Numbering

- **Invoice numbers** follow the prefix and starting number you set in [Invoice Settings](/settings/school-profile) (e.g. `INV-2026-01001`).
- **Receipt numbers** are generated automatically and are always numeric and unique.

<Tip>
Turn on **background reminders** so guardians are nudged about unpaid invoices automatically — see [Notifications](/settings/notifications).
</Tip>
