---
title: "Roles & Permissions"
description: "Understand how roles control what each staff member can see and do."
---

Roles decide which parts of YukiCares each user can access. Assign the least access a person needs to do their job.

## Typical roles

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Administrator" icon="user-shield">
    Full access — settings, users, students, fees, payroll, reports and the audit log.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Accountant / Finance" icon="calculator">
    Fees, invoices, payments, expenses, payouts and financial reports.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Front office" icon="desktop">
    Enrol students, collect fees and print receipts.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Viewer" icon="eye">
    Read-only access to dashboards and reports.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
The exact set of roles available depends on your plan and configuration. Access to money-sensitive actions (recording payments, processing payouts) is always permission-checked on the server.
</Note>

## Good practice

<Steps>
  <Step title="Give admin access sparingly">
    Only people who manage the whole school should be administrators.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use finance roles for money work">
    Accountants get the payment, payout and expense tools without full admin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review access periodically">
    Deactivate accounts for staff who leave and re-check roles each term.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
Sensitive actions are recorded in the **Audit Log** (visible to admins) so you can see who did what.
</Info>
