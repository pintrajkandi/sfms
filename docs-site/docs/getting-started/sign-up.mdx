## Register your school

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sign-up page">
    Visit the YukiCares sign-up page and choose **Register your school**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your details">
    Provide:
    - **School name**
    - **Admin email** (this becomes the first administrator account)
    - **Password**

    The system creates a private workspace for your school and provisions the first admin user.
    ![Create your school's account](/img/sinngup.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Note your subdomain and school code">
    Your school gets:
    - A **subdomain** — e.g. `greenfield.yukicares.cloud` — this is where you and your staff sign in.
    - A **school code** (e.g. `GHS-3000`) — shown on the sign-in screen and used to route you to the right workspace.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
Every school is fully isolated. Staff sign in only from **their own subdomain**, and no data is ever shared between schools.
</Info>

## Verify your email

For security, the first admin is created **unverified**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the verification email">
    We email a secure link (valid for 3 days) to your admin address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the link">
    This confirms your email and enables login.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Login is blocked until your email is verified. If you try to sign in first, you'll see an *"email not verified"* message with a **Resend verification** option.
</Warning>

## What happens next

After verifying, your **first login takes you straight to Settings** to complete your school profile. The rest of the app stays locked until you save your profile — see [Complete your school profile](/settings/school-profile).

<Note>
Signing in with Single Sign-On (SSO)? If your organisation has SSO enabled, use the SSO button on the login screen instead of a password. If it isn't enabled, password login is used.
</Note>
