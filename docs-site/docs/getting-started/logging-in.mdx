## Sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to your school's address">
    Open your workspace subdomain, e.g. `your-school.yukicares.cloud`. If you only know your **school code**, enter it on the sign-in screen and you'll be routed to the right workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email and password">
    Use the credentials for your staff account. Sessions are secure and scoped to your school only.
  </Step>
   ![Login to  school's account](/img/school-login.jpg)
</Steps>

<Tip>
Bookmark your subdomain (or install the app — see [Use on mobile](/mobile-app)) so you land on the right sign-in page every time.
</Tip>

## Forgot your password?

Choose **Forgot password** on the sign-in screen, enter your email, and follow the reset link we send you.

## Can't sign in?

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="'Email not verified'">
    Your account hasn't confirmed its email yet. Use **Resend verification**, then open the link in the email.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="'Complete your settings to continue'">
    New schools must fill in their profile first. You'll be redirected to **Settings** — enter your school name and save. See [School profile](/settings/school-profile).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Wrong workspace">
    Make sure you're on your school's subdomain. Signing in on the wrong subdomain won't find your account. Use your **school code** on the sign-in screen to be routed correctly.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
