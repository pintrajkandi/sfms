## Create a payout

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Teacher Payouts">
    Search and select the teacher — their salary structure pre-fills the amounts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the pay period and amounts">
    Set the **pay period** (month), and confirm base, bonus and deductions. Net pay is calculated as base + bonus − deductions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    The payout is created with status **Pending**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
A teacher can't have two payouts of the same kind for the **same month**. If one already exists, you'll see an alert — *"A salary payout for … already exists for …"*. A **rejected** payout can be re-submitted for that month.
</Warning>

## Review, update status & print a payslip

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Payouts List">
    Every payout is listed with teacher, period, amounts and status. Click a row (or **View payslip →**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the payslip">
    The payslip shows earnings, deductions and **net pay** on your school **letterhead** (logo, name, address).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update the status">
    From the payslip you can **Mark as Paid** or **Reject** (each asks for confirmation).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Print the payslip">
    Choose **Print Payslip** to print or save it as a PDF, letterhead and all.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Full statutory payroll

For a complete salary run with **PF / ESI / TDS / professional tax**, use the payroll run — deductions are computed from your [Payroll settings](/settings/payroll). You can override any statutory component for a special case.

<Note>
Payouts are **idempotent and status-guarded** — a payout moves from Pending to either Paid or Rejected, and can't be double-processed.
</Note>
