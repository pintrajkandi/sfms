## The student list

Open **Students** from the sidebar to see your roster. Use the **search** box to find a student by name, ID, phone or guardian.

<Tip>
Search is fast even on large rosters — type a partial name or ID and results filter instantly.
</Tip>

## Student detail

Click a student to open their profile. You'll see:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Fee summary" icon="indian-rupee-sign">
    **Total fee**, **paid** and **outstanding**, plus a progress bar. Outstanding is based on the student's [assigned annual fee](/students/add-student).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Fee breakdown" icon="list">
    Per fee type: billed, paid and balance.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Payment history" icon="clock-rotate-left">
    Every payment recorded for the student, with method and receipt.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Quick actions" icon="bolt">
    Jump straight into collecting a fee for this student.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Editing a student

Open the student and choose **Edit** to update any field, including their [fee structure](/students/add-student). Outstanding balances recalculate automatically after you save.

<Note>
Students are archived rather than hard-deleted where the workflow implies it, so historical payments and invoices stay intact.
</Note>
