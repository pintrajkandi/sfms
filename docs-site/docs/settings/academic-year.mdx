---
title: "Academic Year"
description: "Define academic years and activate the current one so invoices and enrolments are dated correctly."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Academic Year">
    View the academic years you've defined.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a year">
    Enter a label (e.g. *2025–2026*) and its start/end.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark it active">
    Set the current year as active so it's the default on new invoices and enrolments.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
When collecting fees, you can pick the academic year the payment applies to. Keeping the active year up to date keeps your reports clean year over year.
</Info>
