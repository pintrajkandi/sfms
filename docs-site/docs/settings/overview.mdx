---

Open **Settings** from the sidebar. Settings are organised into tabs down the left of the page. Make your changes and click **Save changes** at the top right.

<Warning>
**New schools must complete their profile first.** Until your school **name** is saved, every screen redirects back to Settings and you'll see a welcome banner. Once saved, the full app unlocks.
</Warning>

## Recommended setup order

<Steps>
  <Step title="School Info + Contact + Branding">
    Fill in your identity and upload your logo so documents look professional. [Guide](/settings/school-profile)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Academic Year">
    Add the current year and mark it active. [Guide](/settings/academic-year)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Classes & Sections">
    Add every class and its sections. [Guide](/settings/classes-sections)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Departments">
    Add departments for your teaching staff. [Guide](/settings/departments)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fee Setup">
    Create your fee types. [Guide](/settings/fee-setup)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Payroll">
    Confirm statutory rates before running payroll. [Guide](/settings/payroll)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invoice Settings + Notifications">
    Set your invoice numbering and turn on reminders. [Reminders](/settings/notifications)
  </Step>
</Steps>
