These tabs live under **Settings** and control what appears on your invoices, receipts and payslips.

## School Info

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → School Info">
    This is the first tab and the one that unlocks the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in your identity">
    - **School name** (required — unlocks the rest of the app)
    - **School type** (e.g. private/public)
    - **Registration number**, **affiliation board**, **established year**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save changes">
    Click **Save changes** at the top right.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Contact Details

Enter your **primary phone**, **official email** and **address** (street, city, state). These appear as the letterhead on printed receipts, invoices and payslips.

## Branding & Logos

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload your logo">
    On the **Branding & Logos** tab, upload your school logo. It appears on the app and on printed documents.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload a letterhead logo (optional)">
    A separate, higher-resolution logo used specifically for printed letterheads.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your brand colour">
    Pick an accent colour used across the app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Uploading a logo + full address is what makes your printed **receipts and payslips look like an official school letterhead**. Without them, documents fall back to a plain header.
</Tip>

## Invoice Settings

Control how invoices are numbered and taxed:

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Invoice prefix** | Text before each number, e.g. `INV` → `INV-2026-01001`. |
| **Starting invoice number** | The first sequence number to use. |
| **Currency** | Your billing currency (₹ INR by default). |
| **Default tax rate** | Applied where GST/tax is relevant. |
| **Invoice footer note** | Free text printed at the bottom of every receipt/invoice. |

<Note>
Receipt numbers are **generated automatically** and are always numeric — you don't set these manually. Invoice numbers follow the prefix/sequence above.
</Note>
