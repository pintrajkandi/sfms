---
title: "Departments"
description: "Create the departments used when registering teachers and staff."
---

Departments group your teaching and non-teaching staff (e.g. *Mathematics*, *Administration*, *Sports*).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Departments">
    View existing departments.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a department">
    Enter the department name and save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
When you [add a teacher](/staff/add-teacher), **Department** is a required field. If you've created departments here, you'll pick one from a dropdown; otherwise you can type it in directly.
</Info>
