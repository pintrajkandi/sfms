---
title: "FAQ & Troubleshooting"
description: "Answers to the most common questions about using YukiCares."
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I registered but can't log in.">
    You likely haven't verified your email. Use **Resend verification** on the sign-in screen and open the link. See [Sign up & verify](/getting-started/sign-up).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The app keeps sending me to Settings.">
    New schools must complete their profile first. Enter your **school name** on Settings and save to unlock the rest of the app. See [School profile](/settings/school-profile).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My printed receipt has no logo or address.">
    Upload your **logo** and fill in your **address** under Settings → Branding and Contact Details. Once set, receipts, invoices and payslips print as a proper school letterhead. See [Invoices & receipts](/fees/invoices-receipts).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How is the receipt number decided?">
    It's generated **automatically** and is always numeric and unique — you don't enter it. Invoice numbers use the prefix/sequence in [Invoice Settings](/settings/school-profile).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which payment methods can I record?">
    **Cash, Bank Transfer and UPI** in the collection wizard, with an optional reference/UTR for non-cash methods. If online payments are enabled, guardians can also pay a balance online from an invoice. See [Collect a fee](/fees/collect-fee).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why can't I create a second payout for the same month?">
    A teacher can't have two payouts of the same kind for the same pay period. Reject the existing one to re-submit, or use a different month. See [Payouts](/staff/payouts).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A teacher's phone number is rejected.">
    Phone numbers must be **numeric and exactly 10 digits**, and unique across teachers. See [Add a teacher](/staff/add-teacher).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I see how much a student still owes?">
    Set the student's **fee structure** (school, tuition, transport, other) on their record. YukiCares then shows **total, paid and outstanding** on the student detail and collection screens. See [Add a student](/students/add-student).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can parents log in?">
    The current version does not include a parent login. Guardians receive fee **reminders** over WhatsApp/SMS and, where enabled, can pay online via a link. See [Notifications](/settings/notifications).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use it on my phone?">
    Yes — install it as an app. See [Use on mobile](/mobile-app).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Still need help?

Reach out from the **Support** section inside the app, or contact your administrator. Include the screen you were on and what you expected to happen so we can help faster.
